# 🎮 Player guide: join your guild and use your Player Portal

The Player Portal is **your** space: tell your officers you took part in an event, keep your stats up to date, and follow your progress. It's free, and you sign in with Discord.

## 🚪 Step 1: Join your guild (one time only)

**You need 2 things:**
- Your guild's **join code** (looks like `FGF-XXXX-XXXX`). Ask your officers: they find it in the tool.
- Your **in-game UID** (your player ID number, shown on your in-game profile).

**Then:**
1. Open the tool and click **Sign in with Discord**.
2. Choose **Join a guild**.
3. Type the **join code** and your **UID**.
4. Click **Request access**.

⏳ Now wait for an officer to **approve** you. When they have, click **Sign in with Discord** again. You're in!

---8<---

## 😕 It didn't work?

- **"That UID is not on the guild roster."** Your officers have not added you to the member list yet. Ask them to add you, then try again.
- **"That join code is not valid."** Check the code with your officers. Letters and numbers only, no extra spaces.
- **"That UID is already attached to another account."** You (or someone else) already used it. Tell your officers.
- **"Your account is awaiting approval."** Be patient: an officer must click **Approve**.

**Next time**, just click **Sign in with Discord**. You have no password to remember.

---8<---

## 📅 Tell your officers you played: Active Events

When your guild starts an event, it appears in **Active Events**.

1. Tick **I participated in this event**.
2. Fill in your score if there is a box:
   - **SvS / GvG**: **Days 1-5 score** and **Day 6 score**
   - Other events: **Score**
   - **Shadowfront**: tick **I was late** if you arrived late.
3. Click **Submit**.

The card now says **Awaiting approval** ⏳. An officer checks it and approves it. Then it says **Confirmed by an officer** ✅.

-# Made a typo? Tell an officer: they can fix it before approving.
-# "Not in this squad"? For Shadowfront, only players picked for that squad can submit.

## 👤 My Info: keep your profile fresh

- **My Military Force Metrics**: your power, Fleet Rating, Technology, Flagship, Champions, Crew and Glory. Update them, then click **Save Military Metrics**. Officers use them to build the best squads!
- **My Timezone**: pick your city, or click **Auto**, then **Save**. Officers can then plan events at a good time for everyone.
- **Discord sign-in**: shows *Linked to Discord* once you sign in with Discord. Still using a password? **Switch to Discord sign-in** moves you over in one step.
- **Request Guild Transfer**: ask to move to another guild.

## 🌴 Absence: going away?

Go to **Absence** → **Declare a period**:
- **Type**: *Full absence* or *Reduced activity*
- **From** / **To**: your dates
- **Note** (optional)

Click **Save**. Your officers will see it, so nobody wonders where you are.

---8<---

## 📈 See how you're doing

- **My Progress**: your scores over time, compared with your guild.
- **Challenges**: small weekly goals and your season level.
- **Badges**: achievements you unlock as you play.

## 🤖 Use the Discord bot

If you sign in with **Continue with Discord**, your Discord account is already linked: the bot knows who you are, so you can check your stats from Discord without opening the tool.

Still signing in with an identifier and password? Go to **My Info** → **Switch to Discord sign-in** once.

Now try `/myinfo` in Discord! 🎉 See the bot guide for all the commands.
