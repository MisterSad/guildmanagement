# 👥 Guide 8: Manage your roster (Members page)

The **Members** page is your guild's member list. Everything else depends on it: events, stats, the Discord bot, players joining. Keep it up to date!

## 🗂️ Two ways to look at it

At the top of **Members list**, switch between:
- **Roster Cards**: one card per player, grouped by rank (R5 → R1). You can see the power, UID, time zone, absences and a **Portal** badge when the player has an account.
- **Military Matrix**: a big table with every stat (Fleet, Tech, Flagship, Champs, Crew, Glory) and a **Rally Score**. The top 16 are marked **Leader #1…#16**. That's your rally-leader list!

**Find someone fast:** use the search box (name or UID), the tier filter (👑 S to 🛡️ D, compared with your strongest player), and the **Sort** menu.

## ➕ Add a player

In **Add a member**:
1. **Username**: exactly as in the game
2. **Player UID**: 6 to 10 digits
3. **Player Power**, then the **Role** (R1 by default)
4. Optional: click **+ Optional Military Scores** to add Fleet, Tech, and so on
5. Click **Add**

✅ The player is also added to events that are running right now.

**Common messages:**
- **"This username is already in the guild!"**: check the list, they're already there.
- **"Player Already in Guild"**: same UID, new name. Click **Update Player Name**.
- **"Player ID already exists"**: the player belongs to another guild. You can send a **transfer request** instead.
- **"Cannot add this player because their UID is banned!"**: see Guide 12.

## ✏️ Edit, move or remove a player

On a card:
- ✏️ **Edit**: name, UID, power, rank, time zone and all military scores. A renamed player keeps their history: the old name is saved in **Name History**.
- ⇄ **Transfer**: move the player to another guild of your server, right away (see Guide 13).
- 🗑️ **Delete**: removes the player.

⚠️ **Deleting a player also deletes their whole history**: participations, scores, squads and sanctions. If a player only changes guild, use **Transfer** instead.
-# The **R5** cannot be deleted, banned or removed by OCR. New leader? Change the old R5's rank with ✏️ **Edit** first.

## 🎖️ Change many ranks at once

Tick several players (or **Select All**). A bar appears at the bottom: **Assign Rank:** R1, R2, R3 or R4.
-# The R5 is protected: it cannot be selected or changed in bulk.

## 📸 Update everyone's stats with screenshots

Click **AI Metric Scanners** and pick a scanner:

- **1. Members OCR**: your full member list. It adds new players, updates power and ranks, and shows who left.
- **2. Power OCR**: power only.
- **3. Fleet OCR**, **5. Flagship OCR**, **6. Champs OCR**, **7. Crew OCR**: from the **game leaderboards**. Fill in the **Guild Tag Filter** (e.g. `[PR1M]`) so other guilds are ignored.
- **4. Tech OCR** and **8. Glory OCR**: from your guild's lists.

Then drop your **screenshots** or a **screen recording** (MP4, MOV or WebM, scroll slowly, max 5 minutes and 64 MB), check the table, and click **Validate & Apply**.

-# Scanners 2 to 8 only update players **already in your list**. New names show **Not in Roster**: add them with **1. Members OCR** first.

## 🛡️ Members OCR: read this before removing anyone

At the bottom of the table, **Removed Members** lists the players the AI did **not** find in your screenshots.

- If your screenshots missed part of the list (under 75% found), the tool **keeps everyone** by default and shows **"Partial scan detected"**. Good!
- Before you confirm, **read the names in "To Remove"**. Anyone missing only because a screenshot was skipped would lose their **whole history**.
- The tool asks again: **Confirm Roster Removals**. Take one second to check.

**Tips for a good scan:**
- Scroll through the **whole** member list, **with the rank headers** ("Executive Manager", "Rank 3 Members"…). The AI uses them to set R4, R3, R2 and R1.
- Screenshots can overlap. Duplicates are merged for you.
- New players found with no UID get a **TEMP** UID. The **⚠ Temp UID** badge and the **⚠️ Temp / Missing UID** filter help you fix them later with ✏️ **Edit**.

## 📊 Other useful sections

- **Pending Transfers**: players coming in or going out. Approve ✓ or reject ✗.
- **Member Absences**: who is away or less active.
- **Timezone Coverage**: what time it is for each player right now. Great for picking event times!
