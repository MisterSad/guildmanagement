# ⚔️ Guide 3: Run an event from start to finish

Every event follows the same 3 steps:
**1. Start** it → **2. Fill in** who played and their scores → **3. End** it.

Go to **Events** (left menu) and pick your event at the top: **SvS**, **GvG**, **Shadowfront**, **DTR** or **Arms Race**.

## ▶️ Step 1: Start the event

1. Click **Start**.
2. A window asks for the **Date (UTC)** and **Time (UTC)** of the event. This time is used for the Discord reminders.
3. Click **Start**.

✅ All your members are added to the event automatically. You will see "*N members imported into the event.*"

-# ⏰ Times are in **UTC** (game time). Not sure? Check the in-game clock.
-# Wrong time? Click the 📅 button next to the event to change it.

**Special cases:**
- **Arms Race** has two tabs: **Stage A** and **Stage B**. Start each one separately.
- **Shadowfront** has **Squad One** and **Squad Two**. First pick your squad in **1. Squad Composition** (up to 20 main players and 10 substitutes). Then track who played in **2. Participation Tracking**.
- **SvS / GvG**: type the enemy server or guild in the **vs** box. Click **D1 … D6** to mark each day as Won (W) or Lost (L).

## ✍️ Step 2a: Fill in by hand

Each member has a line. Tick **Participated** and type the scores.

- **SvS / GvG**: one box per day (**Day 1 … Day 6**) and **Chests**.
  Only have the total? Switch to **Cumulative D1–D5** and type one number.
- **DTR**: tick **Appointed** for players who were assigned. There is no score.
- **Arms Race / Shadowfront**: tick who was there.

💾 **There is no Save button.** Everything saves by itself a second after you type.
-# Typing a score above 0 ticks **Participated** for you.

Use the search box to find a player fast.

---8<---

## 📸 Step 2b: Fill in with screenshots (OCR)

Typing 100 scores by hand is boring. Let the AI read your screenshots!

1. On the event page, click the OCR button. Example: **📅 SvS Day N OCR**, **🔥 SvS Day 6 OCR**, **🚛 DTR OCR**.
2. For a "Day N" scan, pick the day (**Day 1 … Day 5**).
3. Drop your **screenshots** (PNG/JPG), or a **screen recording** where you scroll slowly through the list (MP4/MOV, max 8 minutes).
4. Wait for the AI to read them.
5. **Check the table:**
   - The summary at the top counts the lines **Ready to Apply** ✅
   - **Other Guild / Non-Member** = the AI did not recognise the name. It is unticked. Fix the name by hand and tick it again, or leave it out.
   - You can edit any name or score.
6. Click **Validate & Apply**. Done!

-# ⚠️ The event must be **started** first, or OCR will refuse to apply.

---8<---

## 📷 Tips for good screenshots

- Show the **whole line**: player name **and** score.
- Screenshots can overlap. Duplicates are merged for you.
- Sort the ranking from **highest to lowest**.
- For a video: scroll **slowly and steadily**, and keep the list in the middle of the screen.
- iPhone video fails? Re-export it as **H.264**, or use screenshots.
- Use the **Guild Tag Filter** so players from other guilds on the leaderboard are ignored.

## 👥 Bonus: import your whole roster

On the **Members** page: **AI Metric Scanners** → **1. Members OCR**. Take screenshots of your in-game member list, **with the rank headers** (R4, R3…). The AI adds new players, updates ranks and shows who left.
-# If a scan looks incomplete, removals stay unticked for safety. Check before you remove anyone: removing a member **deletes their history**.

## ⏹️ Step 3: End the event

Click **End**. You will see "*Event saved and ended.*"

- **DTR, Arms Race and Shadowfront end by themselves** 24 hours after they start.
- **SvS and GvG** stay open until you click **End**. Do it at the end of the week!
- Made a mistake after ending? Open **History**, click the event, and fix it there.
- Started by mistake? The 🗑️ button **deletes the session and all its scores**. It cannot be undone!

## 🙋 Players can help

Players can report their own participation in the **Player Portal** → **Active Events**. Their line shows **Pending**. Check the numbers, then click **Approve** (or **Approve All**).

## 📊 Where are the results?

- **History**: every past event, with its scores.
- **Stats**: rankings and each member's reliability.
- **Overview** → **Export Guild Data (.xlsx)**: everything in one Excel file.
