# 🔀 Guide 13: Move players between guilds (transfers)

A transfer moves a player **with their history** (events, scores, sanctions) from one guild to another. It is much better than deleting and re-adding them.

**Who can move where?** Guilds on the **same game server**. Moving to another server only works during a **Migration Window** opened by the developer.

## Way 1: You send a player to another guild (instant)

1. **Members** → on the player's card, click ⇄ (**Transfer**).
2. Pick the guild in **Select Target Guild**.
3. Click **Confirm Transfer**.

✅ Done right away: the other guild does **not** need to approve.
-# Use it for your sister guilds, when you agree with their officers.

---8<---

## Way 2: You want a player from another guild (request)

1. **Members** → **Add a member** → type the player's UID.
2. The tool says **"Player ID already exists"**: the player belongs to another guild.
3. Tick the box, then click **Request Transfer**.

The request appears in **Pending Transfers** (**Members** page).

## Way 3: A player asks to move

In the **Player Portal**: **My Info** → **Request Guild Transfer** → pick the guild → **Send**.

-# One request at a time per player. The player is not notified of the answer, so tell them yourself!

---8<---

## ✅ Approve or reject a request

Open **Members** → **Pending Transfers**:
- **IN** = a player coming **to your guild**. Click ✓ to approve, ✗ to reject.
- **OUT** = a player leaving your guild. You'll see *Awaiting approval from…*: the **receiving** guild decides.

⚠️ There is **no notification** for new requests. Check **Pending Transfers** regularly (see Guide 16).

**Common messages:**
- *"This player has already left their original guild"*: the request is out of date. Reject it.
- *"A member with this UID/name already exists in your guild"*: the player is already in your list. Check for a duplicate.
- *"No other guild is eligible for transfer right now"*: no guild on your server has a server number yet, or no migration window is open. Ask the developer.
