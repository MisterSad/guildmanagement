# 🗒️ Guide 17: Migration Hub, your shared intel notebook

The **Migration Hub** is a **shared notebook** for your officers: agreements, strategy, recruitment, blacklists, intel on other servers… And when you have many notes, the **AI** can summarise them for you.

-# Only admins and officers can see it. Players cannot.

## 🔐 First: who can read what?

At the top, pick where you write:
- **Guild Intel [TAG]**: only **your guild's** officers (plus server admins).
- **Server Intel [1058]**: **every guild's officers on your game server**, including rival guilds!

⚠️ Keep secrets in **Guild Intel**. Use **Server Intel** only for what you want to share with the whole server.

---8<---

## ✍️ Write a page

1. Click **New Wiki Page**.
2. Give it a **title** (e.g. *"State 1089 NAP Agreement"*) and a **category**: *General*, *Strategy*, *Agreements*, *Recruitment* or *Blacklist*.
3. Write your note. The toolbar adds titles, **bold**, lists, quotes and tables.
4. Add **screenshots**: drag them in, or paste them.
5. Tick **Pin to top** for important pages, then **Save Page**.

Other officers can **edit** any page and add **comments** at the bottom. Everything updates live for everyone.

-# Only the **page's creator** can delete it.

---8<---

## 🤖 Let the AI summarise

- **AI Summary** (on a page): a short summary of that page and its comments.
- **AI Digest** (at the top): a **briefing across all the notes** of the current scope, perfect before a migration meeting.

Click **Copy Briefing** to paste it anywhere.

-# The AI reads the text of your notes and comments, never your screenshots.
-# Always double-check an AI summary before you act on it.

## 📤 Share a page in Discord

Click **Discord** on a page: it copies the page as a ready-to-paste message. Screenshots are not included: attach them yourself.

## 💡 Ideas of pages

- 🤝 **Agreements**: NAPs and alliances, with the date and who agreed
- 🎯 **Strategy**: SvS plans, garrison rotations
- 🧲 **Recruitment**: players you're talking to
- ⛔ **Blacklist**: players to avoid, and why
- 🔎 **Intel**: enemy servers, strong players, their habits
