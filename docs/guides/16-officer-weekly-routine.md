# 🗓️ Guide 16: The officer's weekly routine

A checklist to keep your guild's data clean in **15 minutes a week**. Each step links to its guide.

## 📌 Every Monday (new week)

- [ ] **Glory**: enter everyone's Glory, or scan it with **8. Glory OCR** (Guide 10).
- [ ] **Members**: new players or departures? Run **1. Members OCR** and read the **To Remove** list carefully (Guide 8).
- [ ] **Stats → Guild Cockpit**: any **Pending approvals**? Anything missing in **How complete is this data?** (Guide 9)

## ⚔️ During each event

- [ ] **Start** the event with the right **UTC** time. Reminders depend on it (Guide 3, Guide 7).
- [ ] Fill in who played: by hand, or with the event's **OCR** button (Guide 3).
- [ ] Tick **Excused** for players with a good reason, so they're not punished in Stats.
- [ ] **Approve** player submissions (**Pending** lines).
- [ ] **End** the event. DTR, Arms Race and Shadowfront end by themselves after 24 hours. **SvS and GvG don't**: end them yourself!

## 🧹 Every Sunday (5 minutes)

- [ ] **History**: quick look at this week's events. Fix any mistake (Guide 11).
- [ ] In Discord: `/verifications` → players held in `[PENDING]`? Add them in **Members** (Guide 6).
- [ ] **Members → Member Absences**: who is away next week?
- [ ] **Members → Pending Transfers**: approve or reject (Guide 13).

## 🗓️ Once a month

- [ ] Discord: `/reconcile-roles` → remove the guild role from players who left (Guide 6).
- [ ] **Members**: fix the **⚠ Temp UID** players with ✏️ **Edit** (Guide 8).
- [ ] **Overview** → **Export Guild Data (.xlsx)**: keep a backup for yourself.
- [ ] **Accounts & access**: remove accounts of officers who left (Guide 2).

---8<---

## 🤝 Share the work

You don't have to do it all alone:
- Give your R4s their own access (**Guide 2**). Each one can own an event.
- Post an **`/invite`** button for new players, and ask everyone to update their stats with `/update-stats` and declare absences with `/absent` (**Guide 6**).
- Turn on the automatic **Discord reminders** once, and forget them (**Guide 7**).
