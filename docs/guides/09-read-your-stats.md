# 📊 Guide 9: Read your Stats

The **Stats** page answers one question: **who shows up?** It has 3 tabs: **Guild Cockpit**, **Leaderboard** and **Event Deep-Dive**.

## 🎯 First, the key idea: Reliability

Every player gets a **Reliability** score in %. It means: *"out of the events this player could attend, how many did they attend?"*

Big events count more than small ones. By default:
- **SvS: 5**, **GvG: 5**, **Shadowfront: 3**, **DTR: 2**, **Arms Race: 1**

So missing an SvS hurts much more than missing an Arms Race.

**What counts:**
- ✅ **Participated** ticked, or **Sub Present** (Shadowfront) → present
- ❌ Not ticked → absent
- 🟦 **Excused** → that event is **not counted at all**, neither present nor absent
- ⏰ **Late** → still counts as present
- ⏳ **Pending** (a player's submission not approved yet) → not counted until you approve it
- 🏆 **Glory** never counts in Reliability

-# SvS, GvG and Shadowfront count **once per week**. Arms Race and DTR count **once per session**.

## 🧭 Guild Cockpit: your guild at a glance

It always looks at the last weeks, whatever the menus say:
- **Roster**, **Core members** (60%+ reliability), **Dormant** (0%), **Median reliability**
- **Engagement**: how many players are *Core* (60%+), *Regular* (30-60%), *Occasional* (under 30%) or *Dormant*
- **War record**: your SvS and GvG results, day by day (W / L)
- **Power progression**: how your median power grows week after week
- **Members turning up each week**
- **How complete is this data?**: tells you which stats are missing for many players (Fleet, Tech…). Run the matching scanner on the **Members** page (Guide 8).

-# **Pending approvals** appears when players are waiting for your approval. Approve them, or they don't count!

## 🏅 Leaderboard: who is the most reliable?

Pick a **week** and a **period** (1, 2, 4 or 8 weeks, or all time).

- **By event**: `SvS 2/3` means 2 SvS attended out of 3. 🟢 80%+, 🟠 50%+, 🔴 below.
- **Attended**: all events attended out of all possible, with no weights.
- **Reliability**: the weighted score explained above.
- **Trend**: the last 4 weeks compared with the 4 before. ▲ improving, ▼ dropping, *steady*.
- **—** means no event ran for this player in that period.

Click a player to see their history.

## 🔍 Event Deep-Dive: one event in detail

Pick an event (SvS, GvG, Shadowfront, Arms Race…) to see each player's scores and attendance for it. SvS and GvG also show your last wars and their results.

## ⚙️ Change the weights

Think Shadowfront matters more in your guild? Go to **Discord notifications** → **Guild Configuration & Event Coefficients**. Set **SvS Coeff**, **GvG Coeff**, **Shadowfront Coeff**, **DTR Coeff** and **Arms Race Coeff** (0 to 20), then **Save Configuration**.

-# Players see their own Reliability in the Player Portal (**My Progress**), always with the **standard** weights. If you changed yours, their number can differ a little from yours.

## 💡 Make your stats trustworthy

1. **Tick Participated for every event**, even with no score. A score alone does not count as present.
2. **Use Excused** for players who had a good reason. They won't be punished.
3. **Approve player submissions** quickly.
4. **Don't delete players who left.** Use **Transfer** if they moved to another guild. Deleting erases their history.
