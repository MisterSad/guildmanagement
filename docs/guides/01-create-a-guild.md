# 🏰 Guide 1: Create your guild

It takes **2 minutes**, and it is free. You only need a Discord account.

## Before you start

You need:
- Your **guild tag**. It must be 2 to 12 letters, numbers or `_`. No spaces, no brackets. Example: `PR1M`, not `[PR1M]`.
- Your **server number**: 4 digits, for example `1058`.
- A **Discord account that is at least 30 days old**.

-# One Discord account can create **one** guild. If you already have an account in the tool, ask the developer instead.

## Step by step

1. Open the tool and click **Sign in with Discord**.
2. Accept the Discord screen.
3. A **Welcome** window opens. Choose **Create a guild**.
4. Type your **Guild tag** and your **Server number**.
5. Click **Create guild**.

🎉 That's it! Your guild exists, and you are its first admin. You land on the **Overview** page.

**Next time**, just click **Sign in with Discord**. You have no password to remember.

---8<---

## 😕 Something went wrong?

- **"That guild tag is already taken."** Another guild uses this tag. Ask the developer if it is really yours.
- **"Your Discord account is too recent…"** Your Discord account is less than 30 days old. Ask an existing admin for an invitation code instead (see Guide 2).
- **"This attempt expired…"** You waited more than 15 minutes. Close the window and click **Sign in with Discord** again.
- **"Too many guilds have been created today."** Try again tomorrow.

## ✅ Your first 3 steps as an admin

**Step 1: Add your members** ⚠️ *do this first*
Go to **Members**. You can:
- add players one by one in **Add a member** (name, UID, power), or
- import them from screenshots: **AI Metric Scanners** → **1. Members OCR** (see Guide 3).

-# A guild with **no members** is deleted automatically after about 4 weeks. Add your members early!

**Step 2: Let your players in**
Go to **Accounts & access** and find the card **Guild Join Code**. Click **Copy Code** and share it in your guild's Discord.

Each player then:
1. Clicks **Sign in with Discord** → **Join a guild**.
2. Types the **join code** and their **in-game UID**, then clicks **Request access**.

Then **you** approve them: go to **Accounts & access** → **Pending Account Validations** → **Approve**.

-# A player can only join if their **UID is already in your Members list**. That's why Step 1 comes first.

**Step 3: Add your officers**
Give your R4s their own access. See **Guide 2**.

## ⚙️ Optional: connect your Discord server

Go to **Discord notifications** (left menu):
- **Default Discord Webhook URL**: where event announcements are posted. Click **Test Webhook** to check it works.
- **Verification Channel ID** and **Audit / Officers Channel ID**: for the verification bot.
- **Event Channels & Reminders**: one channel and role per event type.

Click **Save Configuration** when you are done.

-# How to get a channel ID: in Discord, open *Settings → Advanced* and turn on **Developer Mode**. Then right-click a channel → **Copy Channel ID**.
