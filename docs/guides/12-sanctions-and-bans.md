# ⚖️ Guide 12: Sanctions and banned players

Two different tools:
- **Sanctions**: a **warning log**. The player stays in the guild.
- **Banned players**: a **blacklist**. The player is removed and cannot come back.

## 📝 Give a sanction

1. Open **Sanctions** (left menu).
2. In **Apply Sanction**, start typing the member's name and pick them from the list.
3. Write the reason in **Comment / Reason** (e.g. *"Missed SvS without notice"*).
4. Click **Apply Sanction**.

At the **3rd sanction** for the same player, the tool shows a **🚨 RECIDIVIST ALERT**. It's a reminder only: nothing happens automatically.

-# The list **Sanctions History** shows every sanction, newest first. Remove one with 🗑️.
-# A sanction does **not** block the player from anything. It's your guild's memory.

## 🚫 Ban a player

1. Open **Banned players**.
2. In **Ban a UID**, type the **Player UID** (and the name and a reason if you want).
3. Click **Ban**.

**What a ban does:**
- The player is **removed from your member list** right away.
- They **cannot be added back**: not by hand, not by OCR, not by a transfer.
- They **cannot join** with your join code.
- The **Discord bot refuses** to give them your guild role.

⚠️ **A ban deletes the player's history** in your guild (events, scores, sanctions), because it removes them from the list. If you only want to keep a record, give a **sanction** instead.

---8<---

## ↩️ Unban

In **Banned Players List**, click 🗑️ next to the UID → **Unban Player**.
The player is **not** put back automatically: add them again in **Members** if you want them back.

## ❓ Good to know

- A ban is **for your guild only**. Other guilds are not affected.
- Sanctions **follow the player** if they are transferred to another guild.
- Sanctions and bans are included in **Overview → Export Guild Data (.xlsx)**.
