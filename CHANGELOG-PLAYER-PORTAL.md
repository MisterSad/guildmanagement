:rocket: **PLAYER PORTAL — THE BIG UPDATE**

The Player Portal has been completely rebuilt. Create your own account, follow your progression with charts, submit your scores, and let your officers know when you are away. Everything lives in one place now. :sparkles:

---

:one: **Create your own account**

1. Open the app and click **"Create a player account"** on the login screen.
2. Enter an **identifier** (3 to 32 characters), a **password** (at least 8 characters), your **in-game UID** and your **guild join code** (ask your guild admin if you don't have it).
3. Submit. Your account is created in **pending** status.
4. A guild admin **approves or rejects** it from the Accounts tab. You can sign in as soon as it is approved. :white_check_mark:

:lock: **Why it is secure**

- The join code is **never stored in plain text** — only a secure hash is kept.
- Each UID can be linked to **only one account**.
- A UID that is not part of your guild's roster **cannot register**.
- Accounts can only be approved by a **guild admin** of your guild.
- Signing in requires your **identifier and password**. The in-game UID is no longer an access key, so nobody else can use your data just by knowing your UID.

---

:two: **A brand new portal**

Once signed in, the portal opens as a full dashboard with the same design as the guild app:

- **My Progress** — your personal stats
- **Active Events** — submit your participation and scores
- **Absence** — declare periods you will be away
- **Account** — power, timezone, transfers

It works on desktop and mobile, and your session stays open when you reload the page. :reload:

---

:chart_with_upwards_trend: **My Progress**

- :bar_chart: **Score evolution charts** for events with scores (SvS, GvG, Glory). Each chart draws a curve of your points over time, with your score shown on every point.
- :calendar: **Period selector** — view **All Time, 8 Weeks, 4 Weeks or 1 Week**. Stats and charts update instantly.
- :medal: **Stat tiles** — events tracked, participations, attendance rate and current power.
- :clipboard: **Participation tiles** for events without scores (Arms Race Stage A & B, Defend Trade Route) with your attendance rate and recent status badges.

---

:trophy: **Active Events**

- :eyes: A summary shows how many events are active, to submit, and submitted.
- :white_check_mark: One clear toggle: **"I participated in this event"**.
- :hourglass: Event-specific details (appointed, late, excused) are optional.
- :1234: Score fields with a **live total** that updates as you type.
- :pushpin: After submitting, the event shows **"Awaiting approval"** and is marked as done. Officers confirm your submission.

---

:calendar: **Absence**

- :beach_umbrella: Declare a **full absence** or a **reduced activity** period (start and end dates, optional note).
- :eyes: Your officers can see it in the guild's Members tab, and a chip appears next to your name.
- :wastebasket: Remove or update your declarations anytime.

---

:clock3: **Timezone**

- :globe_with_meridians: Set your **UTC offset** (-12 to +14) from the Account tab.
- :bar_chart: Officers can see a **coverage view** showing what local hour every player is at right now, to plan events when the most players are available.

---

:information_source: **For guild admins**

- Generate your guild's **join code** from the **Accounts & Access** tab (you can regenerate it anytime).
- Review **pending registrations** and approve or reject them.
- When a player transfers to another guild on the same server, their account **automatically follows** them.
- **Member Absences** and **Timezone Coverage** sections appear in the Members tab so you always know who is away and when your guild is most active.
