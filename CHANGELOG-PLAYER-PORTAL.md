:rocket: **PLAYER ACCOUNTS UPDATE — CREATE YOUR OWN ACCOUNT**

The Player Portal just got a major upgrade: you can now create your own account, sign in with your own credentials, and manage your participation directly. No more relying on officers to do it for you.

:one: **How to create your account**

1. Open the app and click **"Create a player account"** on the login screen.
2. Enter the following:
   - An **identifier** (3 to 32 characters) and a **password** (at least 8 characters)
   - Your **in-game UID**
   - Your **guild join code** (ask your guild admin if you don't have it)
3. Submit your registration.

:two: **What happens next**

- Your account is created in **pending** status.
- A guild admin **approves or rejects** your registration from the Accounts tab.
- You will be able to sign in as soon as your account is approved. :white_check_mark:

:lock: **Why it is secure**

- The join code is **never stored in plain text** — only a secure hash is kept.
- Each UID can be linked to **only one account**.
- A UID that is not part of your guild's roster **cannot register**.
- Accounts can only be approved by a **guild admin** of your guild.

:three: **What you can do once signed in**

- :trophy: **Submit your scores** for active events (SvS, GvG, DTR, Shadowfront)
- :muscle: **Update your power** anytime
- :arrows_counterclockwise: **Request a guild transfer** to another guild on the same server
- :chart_with_upwards_trend: Follow your participation in the guild's events

:information_source: **For guild admins**

- Generate your guild's **join code** from the **Accounts & Access** tab (you can regenerate it anytime).
- Review **pending registrations** in the same tab and approve or reject them.
- When a player transfers to another guild on the same server, their account automatically follows them.
