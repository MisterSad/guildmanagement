:sparkles: **SCOUTING + WEEKLY CHALLENGES — v3**

Two new tools: one for the super admin, one for every player.

---

:new: **What's new**

- :crosshairs: **Scouting (super admin).** Track rival guild rosters over time:
  - Paste a roster (one "pseudo, power" per line, quotes and thousand separators accepted) and capture a snapshot for any guild.
  - See every tracked player's latest power with the growth delta since the previous capture.
  - Open a player's trend to view their full power history.
  - Super admin only: scouting data is never visible to guild admins or players.
- :dart: **Weekly challenges (Player Portal).** A new Challenges tab gives every player goals for the week:
  - Attend 1 / 3 / 5 events this week.
  - Submit your Glory score.
  - Refresh your power.
  - A season summary (last 4 weeks) shows your total events and a Bronze / Silver / Gold season rank, with live progress bars.
