📢 **FGF Guild Management Tool Update — CHANG_V126.0**

Hey everyone! Today I'm excited to ship our dedicated **Members OCR** scanner alongside a big accuracy upgrade to all our metric scanners. Here is what's new:

📸 **Dedicated "Members OCR" Scanner**
- **Full Roster Management**: The new **Members OCR** button (now #1 in your OCR toolbar) is your all-in-one tool for roster changes. Use it to add new recruits with real in-game UIDs, update overall power, and track members who have left the guild.
- **One-Click Cold Start**: When setting up a new guild with an empty roster, the "Members OCR" button instantly imports your entire guild from a single screenshot batch.

📊 **Update-Only Metric Scanners (No Accidental Recruits)**
- **Safe Metric Updates**: The other 7 scanners (Power, Fleet, Tech, Flagship, Champs, Crew, Glory) are now strictly update-only! They update stats for existing members without accidentally adding new players or removing absent ones.
- **"Not in Roster" Alerts**: If a player appears in a metric screenshot who isn't on your roster yet, the review modal clearly highlights them under a **"Not in Roster"** section with a badge reminding you to add them via Members OCR first.
- **Accurate Feedback**: When applying metric scans, you'll see a clean toast summary showing exactly how many members were updated and how many unmatched players were skipped.
