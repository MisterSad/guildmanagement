📢 **FGF Guild Management Tool Update — CHANG_V3.0**

Hey commanders! 👋

I've just launched a major visual and user experience overhaul across the entire platform, transitioning our design system to **Material Design 3.0 (Material You / M3)** blended with an **Apple-inspired Cozy Neutral aesthetic** (warm graphite, titanium, oyster, and refined mineral accents)!

Here is everything new in this release:

---

### ✨ Material Design 3.0 & Cozy Neutral Atmosphere
- **Warm Graphite & Titanium Surface Hierarchy**: Replaced harsh dark contrasts with a layered, eye-friendly palette (`--md-sys-color-surface`, `surface-container-low` to `surface-container-highest`) offering comfortable long-session readability for both daytime and night fleet battles.
- **Material Symbols Rounded Variable Typography**: Integrated Google Fonts *Plus Jakarta Sans* and *Inter* paired with official *Material Symbols Rounded* icons that smoothly transition between subtle outline and active filled states.
- **Frosted Glass & Hairline Elevation**: Subtle `backdrop-filter` frosted glass layering, rounded modern corners (`24px`), and gentle ambient lighting.

---

### 🔐 Re-Imagined, User-Friendly Login & Registration
- **M3 / Apple Segmented Switcher**: Instant switching between **Sign In** and **Register Player** via a sleek pill control right at the top of the card — no more confusing ghost buttons.
- **Unified Sign-In Gateway**: A single, clean login form for everyone! Whether you are a Guild Leader, Super Admin, or Guild Member, the platform automatically validates your identity and guides you straight into your Command Center or Player Portal.
- **Interactive Password Visibility**: Smooth eye toggle to easily verify your credentials before signing in.
- **Direct Score Lookup**: Players submitting individual scores can still access the lightweight lookup modal directly in one click.

---

### 🧪 100% Verified Quality Gate & Zero Regressions
- **All 235 Vitest Unit Tests Passing**: Preserved full database integrity, session calculations, and business logic.
- **All 7 Playwright Browser E2E Tests Passing**: Verified full end-to-end user navigation flows in real browsers.
- **Zero TypeScript Errors & Clean Production Build**: Ready on production deployment.
