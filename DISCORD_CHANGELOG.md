🚀 **New Feature: Instant Roster & Power Updates via Screenshots**
You can now add new members and update your entire guild roster's power in seconds by uploading screenshots directly in the **Members** tab!

📸 **How It Works:**
1. **Upload Screenshots**: In the *Members* tab, click **`Import Members (OCR)`** and drop your member list or guild ranking screenshots (up to 25 screenshots per batch).
2. **Review & Edit Grid**: The tool scans your images and displays a smart verification table showing detected players:
   - 🟢 **New Players** (automatically registered as new guild members)
   - 🟣 **Power Updates** (updates existing member power records)
   - 🟡 **Smart Name Reconciliation** (detects typos, extra spaces, or tags to prevent ghost duplicates!)
   - ✏️ You can edit any username or power value directly in the review table if needed.
3. **Validate & Save**: Click **`Validate & Apply Updates`** to apply all verified changes to your guild!
