:trophy: **UPDATE — BADGES, NEW KPIs AND FIXES**

A few things landed this week. A gamification system in the Player Portal, four new macro views in Stats, and a handful of fixes.

---

:medal: **Badges in the Player Portal**

Players now have an achievements system. 19 badges across four tracks, all computed from data we already track, so nothing to fill in manually:

- :shield: **Ranks** - one badge per in-game rank, from Initiate (R1) up to Commander (R5)
- :hourglass: **Seniority** - 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, 1 year, 2 years in the guild
- :crossed_swords: **Power** - 10M, 50M, 100M, 500M, 1B combat power
- :calendar_spiral: **Participation** - 10, 25, 50, 100 events attended

Earned badges are colored and glow. Locked ones are greyed out, but each still shows the objective, a progress bar and where you are versus the target (e.g. R4/5, 105M/500M). No guessing what to work on next.

---

:bar_chart: **New KPI tabs in Stats**

The Stats page gains four guild-level views next to the existing leaderboards:

- **Guild Health** - total and average power, how much sits in the top 5 and top 10, and the S/A/B/C/D tier split
- **Engagement** - participation rate for the last 8 weeks, players inactive for 2+ weeks, and how reliable the "declared available" players actually are
- **Roster** - role structure (R5 to R1), tenure buckets, and the transfer in/out balance
- **Operations** - pending score approvals, active events, roster completeness, pending transfer requests

---

:hammer_and_wrench: **Fixes**

- **Shadowfront Discord share** was sending both squads no matter which one you were composing. It now shares only the active squad, with the squad name as the embed title.
- **Overview clocks** were squeezing and cutting off member names once you had three or more. They now sit on a fixed grid (3 columns, 2 rows by default) with full names.
- **Calamity Befalls round 3** reminder fired at the old time. The start moved to 05:30 UTC, so the reminder now goes out at 05:20.
- **Overview "Recorded sanctions" tile** is replaced by the guild's total power, which is the number people actually check. The sanctions feed itself is unchanged.
