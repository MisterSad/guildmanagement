:bust_in_silhouette: **PLAYER PORTAL UPGRADE — MY INFO, GLORY, KPIs AND MORE BADGES**

A solid round of Player Portal improvements.

---

:trophy: **My Info tab**

The "Account" tab was renamed to **My Info** and now holds everything you manage about your player, all in one place:

- :crossed_swords: **Power** - view and update your combat power
- :trophy: **Glory** - enter or edit your weekly Glory score, so officers can track it without asking around
- :clock3: **Timezone** - set your availability window
- :twisted_rightwards_arrows: **Guild transfer** - request a move to another guild

The logout button was removed from this page since it already lives in the sidebar. No more duplicate.

---

:bar_chart: **My Progress gets a personal KPI dashboard**

Four new cards sit above your existing stats, and they tell you exactly where you stand in your guild:

- :crossed_swords: **Combat Power** - your power, your rank in the guild, your percentile and how close you are to the guild's strongest member
- :trophy: **Glory** - your score this week, your best ever, and where you rank in the guild
- :calendar_check: **Attendance** - your rate, compared to the guild average, with a breakdown per event type
- :hourglass: **Tenure** - how many days you have been in the guild

---

:medal: **Many more badges**

The badge collection grew to **45**. More milestones to chase, with finely spaced tiers:

- :hourglass: **Seniority** - new intermediate steps, from 1 month up to 2 years in the guild (1, 2, 3, 6, 9 months, 1 year, 18 months, 2 years)
- :dagger: **Power** - new tiers by 25M steps, from 10M up to 300M combat power
- :calendar_spiral: **Participation** - new tiers from 10 up to 1500 events attended
- :trophy: **Glory (new track)** - unlock badges from 1K up to 50M Glory in a single week

A small but important rule: a Glory score of 0 (or empty) never counts toward the Glory badges. Only real positive scores unlock them, and a player with no score is not ranked.

Locked badges still show the target and your progress, as always.
